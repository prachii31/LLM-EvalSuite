
NIVARA BANK
ACCOUNTS AND FEES POLICY

IMPORTANT:
Nivara Bank is a fictional bank created for the
LLM-EvalSuite software testing project.
All rates, charges and policies below are fictional.

1. SAVINGS ACCOUNT

Nivara Bank offers a regular savings account
for individual customers.

The minimum balance requirement for a regular
savings account is Rs. 0.

The savings account earns interest at 3% per
annum.

Savings account interest is calculated on the
daily closing balance and credited quarterly.

2. PREMIUM SAVINGS ACCOUNT

The Premium Savings Account requires a
quarterly average balance of Rs. 10,000.

If the required average balance is not maintained,
a maintenance fee of Rs. 150 per quarter applies.

The Premium Savings Account earns interest at
3.5% per annum.

Interest is credited quarterly.

3. CURRENT ACCOUNT

Nivara Bank offers current accounts for business customers.

The required monthly average balance for a Nivara Bank current account is Rs. 25,000.

If the required monthly average balance is not maintained, a maintenance fee of Rs. 500 per month applies.

Current accounts do not earn interest.
4. ACCOUNT CLOSURE

Customers can request account closure through
a bank branch or the official banking support channel.

An account closed within 30 days of opening
has a closure charge of Rs. 250.

There is no account closure charge when the
account is closed after 30 days.

Outstanding dues must be cleared before
the account can be closed.

5. GENERAL FEE INFORMATION

All charges mentioned in this document are
fictional project values.

The chatbot must not invent additional charges,
interest rates or account conditions.

If a customer asks about a fee or policy that
is not mentioned in the knowledge base,
the chatbot must clearly state that the
information is unavailable.
