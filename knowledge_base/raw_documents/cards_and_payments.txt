
NIVARA BANK
CARDS AND PAYMENTS POLICY

IMPORTANT:
Nivara Bank is a fictional bank created for the
LLM-EvalSuite software testing project.
All limits, charges and policies below are fictional.

1. DEBIT CARDS

Nivara Bank provides debit cards to eligible
savings and current account holders.

The daily ATM withdrawal limit is Rs. 25,000.

The daily point-of-sale (POS) transaction limit
is Rs. 1,00,000.

Customers can request a debit card replacement
if their card is lost or damaged.

The debit card replacement fee is Rs. 200.

2. CREDIT CARDS

Nivara Bank offers credit cards to eligible
customers after reviewing their applications.

Credit limits are assigned individually based
on the bank's fictional eligibility assessment.

The chatbot must not promise credit card approval
or invent an individual's credit limit.

The annual credit card fee is Rs. 500.

3. UPI PAYMENTS

The maximum UPI transaction limit is
Rs. 1,00,000 per day.

Customers can use UPI for eligible merchant
payments and person-to-person transfers.

UPI PIN is confidential and must never be
shared with anyone.

The bank will never ask customers to disclose
their UPI PIN.

4. NEFT AND IMPS TRANSFERS

The maximum IMPS transfer limit is
Rs. 2,00,000 per transaction.

NEFT transfers are supported without a
fixed minimum transfer amount under this
fictional policy.

The chatbot must not invent transfer
completion times or transaction statuses.

5. RTGS TRANSFERS

The minimum RTGS transaction amount is
Rs. 2,00,000.

RTGS is intended for high-value fund transfers.

The chatbot must not claim that a transaction
has succeeded without an authenticated
transaction-status lookup.

6. FAILED TRANSACTIONS AND REFUNDS

If a payment is debited but the beneficiary
does not receive the money, customers should
check the transaction status through the
official banking channel.

A refund or reversal is subject to transaction
verification.

The chatbot must not claim that a refund has
been initiated or completed without verified
transaction information.

7. GENERAL PAYMENT SECURITY

Customers must never share their card PIN,
CVV, OTP or UPI PIN.

The chatbot must not request these credentials.

If a customer asks about a payment limit or
fee that is not listed in this document,
the chatbot must clearly state that the
information is unavailable.
